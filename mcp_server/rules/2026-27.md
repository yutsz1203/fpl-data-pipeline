## Rules the numbers above do not carry

Written from https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help/rules on 2026-11-10, for 2026/27.

### Squad and team

- The starting 11 needs 1 goalkeeper, at least 3 defenders and at least 1 forward.
- The captain's points are doubled. If the captain plays no minutes, the vice-captain is doubled instead. If neither plays, nobody is.
- A starter who does not play is replaced after the gameweek by the first bench player, in bench order, who did play and keeps the formation legal. A goalkeeper is replaced only by the other goalkeeper. A card counts as playing.

### Transfers

- Transfers are unlimited and free until the manager's first deadline. After it, each gameweek adds 1 free transfer.
- Unused free transfers carry over. A manager can hold 5 at most.
- Each transfer beyond the free ones costs 4 points.
- A Wildcard or Free Hit leaves the saved free transfers as they were: 2 saved before the chip is 2 saved the gameweek after. No free transfer is added for that gameweek.
- Selling price: purchase price plus half of any rise since, rounded down to £0.1m. Bought at 7.5 and now 7.8 sells for 7.6. A fall is taken in full.

### Chips

- One chip per gameweek.
- Bench Boost (bboost): the bench players' points count.
- Triple Captain (3xc): the captain's points are tripled.
- Free Hit (freehit): unlimited free transfers for one gameweek, then the squad goes back to what it was. It cannot be played in two gameweeks running.
- Wildcard (wildcard): every transfer in the gameweek is free. The new squad stays.
- There are two of each chip: one for the first half of the season and one for the second. The chip table above gives the gameweek window of each. A first-half chip that is not played is lost.
- Bench Boost and Triple Captain can be cancelled before the deadline. Wildcard and Free Hit cannot.

### Scoring details

- 1 point for playing, 2 for playing 60 minutes or more.
- 1 point for every 3 saves by a goalkeeper.
- Minus 1 point for every 2 goals conceded by a goalkeeper or defender while on the pitch.
- Clean sheet: the player plays 60 minutes or more and the team concedes no goal while he is on the pitch. A goal after he is substituted does not count against him.
- Defensive contribution, 2 points, once per match: a defender needs 10 clearances, blocks, interceptions and tackles. A midfielder or forward needs 12, and recoveries count for them too.
- Bonus: the three highest BPS scores in a match get 3, 2 and 1. Ties share the higher value.
- A red card is minus 3 in total, including any yellow before it. A player sent off still loses points for goals conceded after he leaves.
- The player who wins a penalty or a direct free kick gets the assist if a team-mate scores it directly.

### Deadlines

- 90 minutes before the first kickoff of the gameweek.
